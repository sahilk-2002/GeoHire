Job Description

GeoHire Labs is seeking a visionary Senior Product Designer to lead the evolution of our career transition platform. You will be responsible for crafting end-to-end experiences that empower professionals to find their next high-growth opportunity with speed and confidence.

You’ll collaborate closely with engineering and product leads to define the visual language of "Corporate Modern" — balancing the trust of traditional industries with the momentum of cutting-edge technology.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience designing complex B2B or consumer-facing web and mobile applications.
  • Expertise in Figma, including design systems, auto-layout, and prototyping for developer handoff.
  • Strong understanding of React-based front-end development constraints and opportunities.
  • Proven track record of translating complex user needs into simple, elegant UI solutions.
